# Where to get each sound

Every sound in the [README](README.md) list has a **first pick** and a **backup** here, plus a Freesound search already filtered to CC0 in case neither suits. I chose the picks from their titles, lengths and descriptions, not by listening, so trust your ears over the list.

## The two sources

- **Pixabay** (the picks): free to use in a game, no credit required, free account to download. The one rule: don't redistribute the raw files on their own, which shipping them inside the game doesn't do. Uploads by "freesound_community" are copies of CC0 Freesound sounds.
- **Freesound, CC0 filter** (the "more" links): public domain, no credit required, free account to download. If you take a sound from Freesound outside these links, check the licence on its page and avoid "Noncommercial".
- **Music on Pixabay:** some tracks are registered with YouTube's Content ID, so gameplay videos can get a claim. The game itself is fine. The track page says so if it's registered; skip those if you'll post videos.

Still note every download in `CREDITS.txt` (file name and page link). It costs nothing and settles any question later.

## How to download

1. Open the link, press **Download** and save the file.
2. Rename it to the name in the File column, keeping its own extension (`.mp3` or `.wav`).
3. Drop it in this folder.

Where a pick is a long recording (a walk, a minute of rain), download it as-is. I'll cut the steps, loops and variants out of it.
